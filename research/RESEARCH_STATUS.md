# Research Status — Present-Day Reconstruction

**Status date:** 2026-11-13  
**Target baseline:** the garden as it exists today  
**Important:** this document records source leads and research conclusions. It does not certify a survey or grant rights to reproduce source material.

## 1. What is established

- Official Suzhou sources describe four parts: east, central, west, and the southern residential area.
- The UNESCO property map lists 5.195 ha and a geographic reference coordinate. This is a property-level reference, not a survey datum.
- The Suzhou Development and Reform Commission page describes the central garden as approximately 18.5 mu and the west garden as approximately 12.5 mu, and lists major buildings and attractions.
- The Suzhou garden administration record provides historical and restoration chronology.
- The 1993 remote-sensing paper documents analysis of 1986 aerial photography and a 1:1000 color image. Its land-use figures describe the 1986 state, not the current garden.

## 2. Critical survey-data risk

A 2026 commentary by Liu Run'en argues that the most complete detailed drawings currently known to the author are largely from the mid-20th-century survey work led by Liu Dunzhen's team, and that these drawings do not fully represent later changes or all vertical/detail information. The author further argues that a comprehensive contemporary measured survey is not readily available.

This is a specialist commentary, not an official survey inventory. Treat it as a high-priority warning and verify directly with the garden management office, conservation authorities, libraries, and the cited publications.

**Project consequence:** do not assume that a current, complete, downloadable CAD plan exists. P0/P1 must establish a source hierarchy and may need to combine historic measured drawings, historic aerial evidence, official present-day photographs, and authorized on-site capture.

## 3. Priority research publications

### A. Liu Dunzhen, _Suzhou Classical Gardens_ (1979)
A foundational measured-survey monograph. Bibliographic records identify a 479-page edition with photographs and measured line drawings. Obtain a lawful physical or library copy and inspect the Zhuozhengyuan section, including plans, elevations, sections, rockery, water, planting, and architectural details.

### B. _Jiangnan Gardens_ (2021)
A survey-drawing collection reportedly compiling work from the 1950s onward, including selected 2007 and 2015–2017 student surveys. Determine which drawings specifically cover the Humble Administrator's Garden and whether they are sufficiently documented to georeference.

### C. _Suzhou Classical Garden Construction Record_ (2024 PDF)
The index identifies measured drawings for selected garden buildings. Verify the original drawing source, date, measurement method, scale, and rights before using any sheet as geometry.

### D. 1986 aerial photography / 1993 remote-sensing paper
Useful for a historical top-down comparison of water, building footprints, paths, and vegetation. It must be kept in a historical layer and must not be treated as the present-day site plan.

## 4. Source hierarchy for geometry

1. Current authorized survey / conservation drawings with scale, datum, and date.
2. Documented measured drawings with known survey date and scale.
3. Georeferenced aerial imagery or orthophoto with known acquisition date and control points.
4. Official dated photographs and authorized field captures.
5. Scholarly descriptions and historic maps.
6. Tourism maps, generic web photos, and AI-generated images — context only, never measurement authority.

## 5. Immediate research actions

- Ask the Suzhou garden management office whether current measured drawings, conservation records, or a digital twin / point cloud can be consulted for research.
- Inspect Liu Dunzhen's 1979 monograph and the 2021 _Jiangnan Gardens_ atlas for the garden's measured sheets.
- Identify which drawings correspond to today's east, central, west, and residential areas; do not assume historical names map one-to-one to present-day labels.
- Compare historical drawings with dated official photographs and recent field capture.
- Obtain permission before photography in restricted areas or use of scanning equipment.
- Keep every source, coordinate transform, and inference auditable.

## 6. Current conclusion

We have enough official and scholarly source leads to start the research inventory and named-feature register. We do **not** yet have enough evidence to establish a present-day 1:1 local coordinate model or to claim that the complete garden can be reconstructed from public online plans alone.
